# Me2U: 20 Signup Ad Copy Variations

Text-only ad copy for a one-week signup campaign aimed at adults in Nigeria. These are conversion-focused drafts, not a guarantee of registrations within seven days. Measure completed sign-ups rather than link clicks.

**Destination:** <https://app.me2ulend.online/register>  
**CTA for every variation:** Sign Up

## Before launch

- Account activation requires identity checks and a registration deposit. Keep those requirements clear on the signup page before people begin registration.
- These ads do not promise loan approval, a borrowing limit, referral payout, investment return, or a specific financial outcome. Keep it that way unless the relevant live terms and availability are verified.
- Borrowing access and limits depend on eligibility. Review current terms and applicable charges before confirming a transaction.
