# Me2U Ecosystem Vision and Mission

## Vision

A world where love builds trust, people support one another, and communities grow together.

## Mission

Me2U brings people together to build trusted relationships, give and receive meaningful support, and grow through a shared community. We are building Me2U currency to become more useful and valuable as trust, participation, and the ecosystem grow.

## Currency ambition

Me2U aims for the currency's usefulness, adoption, and market value to grow alongside the ecosystem. This is an ambition, not a promised return: its market price may rise or fall, and ecosystem growth does not guarantee price appreciation.

## Messaging guidance

- Keep love, trust, support, and shared progress at the center of public-facing brand copy.
- Describe increasing Me2U currency value as an ambition, never as guaranteed price growth or income.
- Explain registration eligibility, referral rewards, token withdrawals, fees, and market risks in separate product terms before those features are offered.
